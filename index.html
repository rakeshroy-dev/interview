    QUESTIONS TO ASK THE INTERVIEWER

    About the role

    • What does success look like in this role at the 3-month and 12-month mark?
    • What's the biggest backend/technical challenge the team is dealing with right now?
    • What does the on-call or production-incident process look like here?

    About the team & engineering culture

    • How are technical decisions made — RFCs, tech lead call, team consensus?
    • What does the code review and release process look like end to end?
    • How much of the stack is legacy vs actively being re-architected?

    About growth

    • What does the path from this role to senior/staff look like on this team?
    • How is performance measured and reviewed here?
    DAY-BEFORE CHECKLIST
    • Re-read the job description and map 3 resume bullets to it directly.
    • Skim the company's engineering blog / recent news for one talking point.
    • Re-run through the Resume Deep-Dive tab — assume they will probe specifics.
    • Have 2 strong project stories ready that aren't payroll (breadth signal): the chat app and the ticketing system.
    • Prepare your own 2–3 questions from the list above, picked for this specific company.
    • Test your mic/camera 10 minutes early for virtual rounds.
    COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
    • Don't answer "tell me about yourself" with a full resume walkthrough — hit the arc, not every line.
    • Don't say "we" for something you personally built — be precise about your individual contribution.
    • Don't leave a technical answer at theory — always land on how you've actually used it.
    • Don't guess on a technical question — say what you'd check/verify instead of inventing an answer.
    • Don't skip asking questions at the end — it signals genuine interest.
